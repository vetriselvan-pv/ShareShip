## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for a couples and family collaboration app, embodying a warm, friendly, and deeply human aesthetic. The emotional response is one of calm, reliability, and emotional closeness. We utilize a refined Modern Minimalism style combined with soft tactile touches—generous whitespace, organic roundedness, and gentle ambient depth that make shared planning feel like a nurturing experience rather than a chore.

## Colors

The palette grounds the user in nature and tranquility. The soft sage green serves as the primary action and anchor color, complemented by a warm cream background that removes the sterile feel of pure white. Subtle gold accents highlight meaningful moments, achievements, and synchronous milestones, while deep charcoal text ensures high legibility and a gentle, non-aggressive reading experience.

## Typography

We employ Plus Jakarta Sans for its warm, soft-geometric letterforms that balance readability with approachability. Scale is intentionally restrained to maintain an intimate, human cadence. Headlines use tighter tracking for a modern, cohesive feel, while body copy prioritizes open apertures and comfortable line heights for effortless scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid system governs the layout, adapting gracefully from compact mobile viewports to expansive tablet and desktop layouts. We rely on a generous spacing rhythm to foster a sense of calm and breathing room. Content containers utilize comfortable padding to avoid visual clutter, ensuring shared calendars, lists, and memories remain the focal point.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is communicated through ambient, low-opacity shadows tinted with the primary sage and charcoal tones rather than stark black. This creates a soft, floating effect that feels organic and tactile. Surfaces stack intuitively using tonal layering, lifting interactive elements slightly above the warm cream background to invite touch.

## Shapes

The shape language relies on a friendly, rounded aesthetic. With a baseline roundedness of 0.5rem and larger containers scaling up to 1rem and 1.5rem, sharp corners are entirely avoided. This creates an inviting, non-threatening environment where buttons, avatars, and cards feel soft to the touch.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary actions utilize solid sage green with soft rounded corners and generous horizontal padding. Secondary actions use ghost styles or warm cream fills with charcoal text. Hover states elevate subtly with gentle shadow expansion.
- **Chips:** Pill-shaped filter and tag elements featuring soft background tints, ideal for categorizing family members, event types, or task priorities.
- **Lists:** Clean separation utilizing subtle dividers or floating card rows with generous vertical touch targets, ideal for shared grocery lists and daily itineraries.
- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons:** Custom rounded inputs featuring a smooth transition into the primary sage green fill, marked by a crisp cream checkmark.
- **Input Fields:** Soft cream or white background fields framed by low-contrast borders, turning to sage green on focus with gentle floating labels.
- **Cards:** Generously rounded containers elevated with ambient shadows, designed to group related shared events, memories, or household tasks into digestible modules.
- **Avatars:** Circular profile indicators featuring soft gold or sage gradient rings to denote active family members and contributors.
